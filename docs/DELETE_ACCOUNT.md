## In the app
Open **Us → Delete account → Confirm**. This removes your sign-in account and erases the entire shared home, including notes, rounds, earned hearts, decorations, and reunion date. Your partner’s sign-in account remains, but the shared home disappears for both of you.

To erase only the home while keeping your account, choose **Us → Leave and erase shared home**.
